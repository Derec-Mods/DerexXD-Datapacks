# Diverse Shipwrecks

**Diverse Shipwrecks** is a Minecraft datapack that completely reimagines shipwreck generation by breaking ships into modular jigsaw sections and dynamically combining them into hundreds of unique combinations.

---

## Features

- **Procedural Shipwrecks:** Ships are generated in modular sections (bow, midsection, stern) chosen from a diverse pool of degraded, damaged, and sunken variations.
- **Vertical Wrecks:** Includes upward-pointing and downward-pointing vertical shipwrecks that create distinct navigation and salvage challenges.
- **Surrounding Debris:** Wreckage debris and disconnected pieces can spawn around heavily damaged vessels.
- **Wood Variety:** Ships generate across all standard vanilla wood types.
- **Vanilla Loot Intact:** Loot chest placements are dynamically adapted without overriding vanilla loot tables.

---

## Origin & Credits

This datapack is a modernized successor to the original [**Revamped Shipwrecks**](https://modrinth.com/datapack/revamped-shipwrecks) project created by **Wpgradford** and **WightofShadows** (originally shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).

### Why this successor was created:
- The original datapack was left unmaintained at Minecraft 1.21.x for over 2 years without updates.
- Internal datapack and worldgen structure formats evolved significantly in subsequent Minecraft releases, causing the original pack to break completely on modern versions (starting from 26.3)
